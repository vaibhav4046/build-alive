# Prior art for Tandem (read 2026-11-08)

State precisely: the need (company while working) is real and served on screens; the AR desk-companion implementation is poorly served, with research prototypes and adjacent products. It is not "unsolved" and Tandem is not the first to put a companion in AR.

| Comparator | What it is | Link | How Tandem differs (honestly) |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Study With Me" videos | Pre-recorded or live videos of someone studying; YouTube reported more than 520M views of videos with "study with me" in the title in 2023 | https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/study-with-me/ | Same desire, on a flat screen. Tandem uses no screen space and has no ads, but a real person on video may feel more real. Demand evidence is for the videos, not for AR. |
| Focusmate | Live human body doubling over video, scheduled sessions | https://chadd.org/?p=33265 (CHADD overview of body doubling) | Humans, scheduling, camera on. Tandem is instant and private. A human partner remains stronger socially. |
| FLOWN (virtual body doubling platform) and its survey of 117 adults (vendor survey, promotional) | Live virtual sessions; self-reported focus gains | https://neurodiversity.org/?p=11449 | Vendor data, not independent. We claim nothing about outcomes. |
| "You Are Not Alone: Designing Body Doubling for ADHD in Virtual Reality" (arXiv preprint, not peer reviewed, 12 participants) | VR study comparing no double, human double and AI double on a bricklaying task | https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12153 | VR, not AR; tiny sample. Closest research precedent; supports "worth testing", not "works". |
| Mixed-reality body doubling roadmap for adults with ADHD (2026 paper reported by search; not read in full) | Roadmap saying MR prototypes are limited and studies are needed | https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851 | Indicates the AR/MR slot is thinly served; verify before quoting. |
| Peridot Beyond (Niantic) on Snap Spectacles | AR virtual pet and AI companion/guide for outdoor use | https://nianticlabs.com/news/peridot-beyond-multiplayer | Direct AR-companion precedent on the same platform. Different job (outdoor play and guide) and AI-based. Tandem is a seated work-rhythm companion with no AI claim. |
| StudyStream | Live study streams and Discord community; self-reported four million users | https://techcrunch.com/?p=2213744 | Company figure; community of real people. |
