# Product

## Register
Product (the Lens is a tool people work in). The film, thumbnail and README hero are brand surfaces.

## What it is
BUILD ALIVE is a pixel-art game world on Snap Spectacles (shown in the Lens Studio Preview) in which a person watches real AI-coding-agent sessions as little workers on a harbor island, understands each step in plain words, digs into the exact evidence on demand, and, with a separate approval, starts and finishes a permitted task.

## Users
Developers who run coding agents and cannot read the terminal flood; people learning how agents work, down to a child watching beside them. They are seated at a desk, hands free, eyes up.

## Purpose
Make an invisible, fast process visible and learnable without hiding the truth. Simple words for the story, exact evidence one pinch away.

## Tone
Warm, playful, calm, precise. A friendly harbor game, never a toy that hides facts. Plain verbs, sentence case, no jargon in the simple voice.

## Anti-references
Debug dashboards; neon dark-mode hacker screens (dark is invisible on an additive display); Minecraft look-alikes; glass cards and gradients as decoration; emoji icons; floating browser windows; any claim that a model's private thoughts are visible.

## Principles
1. World first, text on demand.
2. Every movement comes from a real event or is visibly marked replay or sample.
3. One truth, three voices (little, maker, engineer).
4. Nothing is written without a separate approval from the person.
5. Seated, comfortable, inside the glasses' field of view.

## Constraints
Learn AR with SPECS rules: a real Lens in the Lens Studio Preview, built with CLAD, the Lens Studio MCP and LEAF; a video under three minutes showing it working. Additive display: black is transparent, so bright fills and light text. Free to run: no paid services, no provider passwords or API keys stored.
