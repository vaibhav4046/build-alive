3. Learner Mode: How AI Works! 🎓 EASY LEARNING
Want to know how AI & Harness Engineering work inside? Here is the simple secret behind what you see on screen:
① Why We Give AI a Checklist (Prompt & Skills)
Imagine building a LEGO castle without instructions—you might put blocks in the wrong place! Skills are like LEGO instruction books that tell the AI the right steps before it starts.
② Why AI Calls Helper Friends (Sub-Agents)
If one robot tries to read 100 books at once, its head gets messy! So the Main AI calls a Helper Bot to check one part and report back a short, clean answer.
③ The "Check Your Homework" Loop (Loop Engineering)
Smart AI never says "I'm done!" right away. It runs a Test Loop (like checking your math homework). If 1 answer is wrong, the loop makes the AI fix it first!
④ Why You Are Always the Boss (Safety Gate)
The AI is your helper, not the boss. Before it runs an important command, it shows a green Approve button and waits for your thumbs-up!
